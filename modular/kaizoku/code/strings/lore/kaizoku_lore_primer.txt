	<p style="text-align:center; color: #999; font-size: 0.95em; margin: 16px 0 20px 0;">
		<strong>CHAPTER 1: THIRD WAR OF HUNDRED SUNS</strong>
		<p><br>Weary by the Demnorian invasions, a bold campaign was launched by Grenzelhoft to take back Heartfeltean lands, as a fresh conquest might rekindle the morale. However, the attack had consequences with Fog Islands rising in defense of its allies with the imperial fleets. Now, the two empires clashes over their sphere of influence on every edge, in the third war of the hundred suns.</p>

	<h2 style="text-align:center;">AREAS OF GRIMORIA</h2>

	<details class="section" open>
		<summary>The Fog Islands</summary>
		<div class="details-content">
			<img class="lore-image small" src="pasghetti8811_warning_poster3.png" alt="Fog Islands Poster">
			<div class="image-caption">Artist: pasghetti8811</div>

			<p class="faction-desc"><strong>The Fog Islands</strong> <em>- "The orbiting menace that brings delirium and obscurity to the unseasoned eyes, a forecasting of destruction anywhere it passes, bringing a heavy fog with hidden warriors that leaves few spared from its widespread piracy."</em></p>
			<p class="faction-desc">A relic of times ancient, the Fog Islands is a moving archipelago in the seas of Grimoria, constantly on the move and ruled by the ceremonial son of Abyssor and spiritual leader Heavenly Emperor Abyssaya. Its body was raised as Abyssor called forth its creation, all to become the home of his spawn.</p>

			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Points of Interest</em></summary>
				<div class="details-content">
				<p class="faction-desc"><strong>Pelangic Empire</strong></p>
				<p class="faction-desc">Fog Islands is home to the Pelangic Empire under control of the Abyssaya Dynasty, the nation is a thalassocratic meritocracy, where social standing is determined by the military service of their clan rather than direct birthright.</p>
				<p class="faction-desc">The population were driven out by Grenzelhoft aggression to resume their ancient traditions of raiding, working to prevent demonic corruption from extending outside its boundaries, and actively participating in the defense of their allies and sphere of influence, Heartfelt, against pressure from other greater powers.</p>
				<p class="faction-desc">As any empire worth its salt, Fog Islands is a concoction of cultures, native or assimilated, with plenty of divide-and-conquer population management, suppression of 'undesirables' and fear-inducing doutrines to keep the empire stable and avoid insurgencies, while providing citizenship privileges no other empire can reliably provide.</p>
				<p class="faction-desc">Outsiders often come to this realm under the ideals of meritocracy, as any <span class="highlight">non-desecrant citizen</span> has a shot to become something more, regardless of their background, something mainlander feudalism does not provide.</p>
					<p><strong>- Heavenly Towers</strong><br>Viewable anywhere on the Fog Islands, these mega-structures were assembled over centuries: monolithic columns held by chains that connect to mountains and ground. The towers are temples dedicated to Abyssor's beloved skies, and Muqians dwell within their maze-like layouts.</p>
					<p><strong>- Mirehaven</strong><br>A flooded grassland in a semi-tropical area with abundant fauna, tall reeds, and varying marshes. Several Undine communities and the LinYou live there. It is well-known for producing rattan and leather. The LinYou use their jungle terrain for ambush tactics, fishing, hunting, and herbal trade, honoring Abyssor's manifestations in the mist. The dampness of the jungle is kept at bay by their armor made of rattan stitched with iron.</p>
					<p><strong>- Dunes of Cinder</strong><br>Arid wasteland with lakes of magma and sand that is home to the Dustwalkers, a nomadic group of abyssariads skilled in horse archery and magma forging. They use hit-and-run tactics and reside in mobile camps. They move from forge to forge, producing inexpensive, cokeless cast iron, and their light armor guarantees that in battle, they have quick-hit tactics that mirror the sandy whirlwinds, appearing from nowhere, striking suddenly, and disappearing before retaliation.</p>
					<p><strong>- Outskirts</strong><br>Large plains, kept in good condition by aqueducts, utilized by the Shuhen people to cultivate wheat and rice. The Shuhen are fierce agricultural fighters who establish militias during times of conflict, wear masks that symbolize their identities, and use farming implements as weapons. They saw the most warfare among the others against the demonic forces and the Grezenholft, and their many tactics and landowning influence grew exponentially.</p>
					<p><strong>- The Destroyed Atoll</strong><br>Once a rendezvous point for raiding trade routes from other empires. Foglander Coastal pirates focused on raiding for knowledge, iron, and steel. Ever since the Apotheosis war, the Great Atoll, is now in ruins. The land is made of jagged rocks and underwater reefs, but the Foglanders have adapted by creating platforms, rope bridges, and silted homes. They rely on rainwater, fishing, and small-scale farming.</p>
					<p><strong>- The Wild Isles</strong><br>Conquered colonial isles used as scouting ports and gateways for mining operations. Psyjin natives still live there.</p>

The Grenzelhofts proudly assimilate all those who come under their heel into their Empire, integrating national identities into their fold and destroying them with their own. Bearing the largest armies in the world, which come under vast scrutiny and disgust as they become increasingly desperate to upkeep.</div>
			<div class="faction-desc">The Grenzelhoftian couldn’t explain a time when the Empire wasn’t at war, and many have served within its military forces to escape the intense poverty of its lower class. Exhibiting a strong sense of pride, mainly towards their own nationality, those outside its realms have likely good reason to escape the encroaching iron grip of house Grenz. Protected by the massive, frigid mountain range to the deep west from the frozen winds of the Unknown Continent: the Zybantu Ziggurat is known for its extremely favorable climate to the production of many commodity goods within the world, leading to their rise as a nationalized identity.</div>

A reawakening that has seen the near complete autonomy of the state by that of the powerful Merchants Guild, which funds nearly every aspect of the Mansa Padasha’s wishes. 	<h2 style="text-align:center;">ABYSSANCTUM AND THEOLOGY</h2>

	<details class="section">
		<summary>Abyssanctum</summary>
		<div class="details-content">
			<img class="lore-image small" src="guayo_namunamu.png" alt="Abyssanctum">
			<div class="image-caption">Artist: Guayo</div>
			<div class="faction-desc">;wip;The core identity of Abyssanctum is that natives are part of Abyssor, non-natives are the absorbed. The objective is to avoid corruption that strays one away from Abyssor, and 'grow back' into Abyssor. is the ritual-martial spine of Fog Island theology: a historical order of warrior-priests, jurists, and sea-commanders that codified Abyssariad discipline after successive schisms. Their doctrine branches into four sacred traditions.</div>
			<div class="faction-desc">What most differences Abyssanctum from the Pantheon, is that the faith is found on every glimpse of existence. Prayer goes not only to honor the Gods and the Thalassic Angels, but to purge unseen corruption, as prayer often comes as sanctification for food before eating, both to ensure no poison or intestinal infection will come, but to request so there will be no lack of food in the future. The very same goes before sleeping, and every funeral is an exorcism on its own way, to ensure the dead will not stay under the effects of undeath.</div>


			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Purifier Doctrine</em></summary>
				<div class="details-content">
					<div class="faction-desc">During the age of Blood Apotheosis's, the armies of Graggar reached Fog Islands as warned by Abyssor's Grey Dream, and the first effective resistance came from those who saw the assaults during sleep, or heard talking spirits during prayer. Though dismissed as hysteria, the recurring visions preceded attacks, and the Elite champion Kimura Tagahara, currently reborn as Quintessential the Nautilus, not only treated these dreams as assets for war, but turned the wartime pragmatism as doctrine for the army, the 'Dreamer Philosophy' emerged ever since, evolving under the ideals of spiritual purity and ascension.</div>
					<div class="faction-desc">The philosophy branched as its thinkers grappled the nature of demonic corruption during war, regarded as a constant threat inherent to the mortal coil as a natural state of creation, light and dark bound in tension, an parasite fed by sin that forever anchors the body and soul, and to weaken it, one must starve it through discipline, restraint and deliberate restoration of order, allowing devouts to perform astral projections while in a state of stupor and interact with the spiritual realm, contacting the dead and lingering ancestors before reincarnation takes place.</div>
					<div class="faction-desc">The philosophy became part of Abyssanctum religion organically ever since the death of Kimura Tagahara during the siege on Sora no Shiden, reborn as thalassic angel rather than reincarnated as a mortal similarly to the other two resonances of the depths. Further discoveries about purification were made after centuries, the doctrine performing restoration of order for the sake of purification, as corruption also comes from accumulation of violence, resentment and prolonged proximity to demonic presence, and whoever this impurity festers, malignant entities come to feast.</div>
					<div class="faction-desc">It is with rites using purifying materials such as salt, cinnamon, burning mizuwood incense, silver dust and ofundas, one is able to restore the natural circulation of spirits, not with the intention of detaching the corruption, but overpowering and ensuring it can no longer resonate with its vessel. Regardless, purifiers should be always willing to punish evil spirits by unarmed combat and rites, as souls can be subdued by a pure soul.</div>
				</div>
			</details>

			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Curator Doctrine<	em></summary>
				<div class="details-content">
					<div class="faction-desc">The preservationist tradition dedicated to maintaining sacred knowledge, artifacts, and the historical continuity of Abyssariad civilization. Curators guard libraries, reliquaries, and ancestral records against loss and desecration.</div>
				</div>
			</details>

			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Tideweaver Doctrine</em></summary>
				<div class="details-content">
					<div class="faction-desc">The maritime tradition bound to oceanic forces and Abyssor's will. Tideweavers harness the power of tides, navigate the realms between water and land, and commune with sea-spirits in service of conquest and sovereignty.</div>
				</div>
			</details>

			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Silent Doctrine</em></summary>
				<div class="details-content">
					<div class="faction-desc">The covert tradition of assassins, spies, and judicial executioners who operate without fanfare under the veil of silence. The Silent serve as instruments of hidden justice and territorial control, bound by absolute discretion.</div>
				</div>
			</details>
		</div>
	</details>

	<details class="section">
		<summary>The Tennites - The Ten Divine Thrones</summary>
		<div class="details-content">
			<div class="faction-desc">Tennite doctrine recognizes ten supreme divine seats shaping mortal fate through law, tide, death, harvest, war, and celestial order. Regional liturgies differ, but the canonical frame remains tenfold.</div>

			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Deities</em></summary>
				<div class="details-content">
					<p><strong>Astrata</strong> - The goddess of harvest, fertility, and agricultural blessing. Her domains span growth, sustenance, and the cycle of seasons.</p>
					<p><strong>Noc</strong> - The god of night, secrets, and hidden knowledge. Noc oversees mysteries, forbidden lore, and the boundary between darkness and light.</p>
				</div>
			</details>

			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Forces</em></summary>
				<div class="details-content">
					<p><strong>Necra</strong> - The keeper of souls and mistress of the afterlife. Necra is neither evil nor good, simply inevitable. Her priests oversee funerals and guide the dead, but some delve into forbidden necromancy, seeking to defy her domain.</p>
					<p><strong>Dendor</strong> - The force of harvest's decay and winter's death. Dendor represents necessary endings, rot as renewal, and the dark cycle of consumption and regrowth.</p>
					<p><strong>Abyssor</strong> - The ancient deity of the ocean, storms, and the unknown depths. Worshipped by the Abyssariads, Abyssor demands honor, loyalty, and sacrifice. His wrath manifests as tidal waves and hurricanes, while his blessing grants power over water and shadow.</p>
				</div>
			</details>

			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Saints</em></summary>
				<div class="details-content">
					<p><strong>Malum</strong> - The primal force of ancestral strength and dwarvish resilience. Malum embodies the foundation of mountains and the unbreakable bonds of clan.</p>
					<p><strong>Ravox</strong> - The voice of prophecy and fate's messenger. Ravox speaks through omens, dreams, and the threads of destiny visible only to shamans and seers.</p>
					<p><strong>Eora</strong> - The spirit of crafted beauty and artistic excellence. Eora blesses makers, builders, and those who elevate the mundane to the sublime.</p>
					<p><strong>Pestra</strong> - The hunger-spirit of plague and suffering endured. Pestra tests mortals through hardship, teaching wisdom through trial and pain.</p>
					<p><strong>Xylix</strong> - The trickster of crossroads and choices unmade. Xylix embodies chaos, chance, and the paths not taken in life's journey.</p>
				</div>
			</details>
		</div>
	</details>

	<details class="section">
		<summary>Psydonic Path (Psydon)</summary>
		<div class="details-content">
			<div class="faction-desc">The god of light, law, and retribution. Psydon is worshipped by paladins, judges, and those who seek order in a chaotic world. The Psydonic tradition exalts justice, oath-keeping, and social hierarchy under divine scrutiny. Its judges and templars preach that mercy is lawful only when duty is preserved.</div>
		</div>
	</details>

	<details class="section">
		<summary>Demons of Grimoria</summary>
		<div class="details-content">
			<div class="faction-desc">Beyond the pantheon of reverence dwell forces of pure corruption—entities worshipped not out of faith, but fear and pursuit of their dark gifts. These Inhuman powers pervert the world and claim followers through promises of power and dominion.</div>

			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Graggar, the Wrath-King</em></summary>
				<div class="details-content">
					<img class="lore-image small" src="pasghetti8811_warning_poster2.png" alt="Graggar">
					<div class="image-caption">Artist: pasghetti8811</div>
					<div class="faction-desc">Graggar is treated as an Inhumen force of predatory ruin: worship through blood, domination, and desecration. His followers seek authority by proving they can break what others still revere. Orcs and Goblins carry his taint through their raids and carnage.</div>
				</div>
			</details>

			<details class="section">
				<summary><em>Zizo, the Hunger-Plague</em></summary>

	<details class="section">
		<summary>Tieflings</summary>
		<div class="details-content">
		<div class="faction-desc">Tieflings, also known as Infernal-Spawn, are one of the younger peoples of Grimoria, appearing during the time of the Apotheosis War. Said to be the offspring of mortals who served Zizo and mated with their Spawn in unholy union, legions of them served as the chattel army under the banners of Zizo and Graggar. After their crushing defeat, Tieflings are said to have begged for mercy and were begrudgingly accepted, after abandoning their Lord.</div>
		<div class="faction-desc">Facing much persecution and prejudice, these people have survived pogroms with remarkable resilience , many seeking a solitary and nomadic life without relying on the wider society to survive. Tieflings are less capable of creating offspring than most peoples, and only produce more tieflings due to their strong Zizo taint, so no half-breeds exist. Most of them tend to be extremely perceptive and paranoid, as luck is rarely on their side and their peculiar anatomy with somewhat fragile, crystalline bones makes them susceptible to injury.</div>
		</div>
	</details>

	<details class="section">
		<summary>Denmorian</summary>
		<div class="details-content">
			<div class="faction-desc">The Denmorian are the unholy amalgamation of beast and man, spawned from Zizonic mana during the struggle with the wretched Warlock. Boasting innumerable variations of beastial features, their integration into society ranges from tremendous successes to horrible failures.</div>
			<div class="faction-desc">Their worldwide invasions left scars on greater empires, as Denmorian warbands broke the civilizations once thought impenetrable, leaving people desperate and homeless, filling islander empires with refugees, and their own traitors… or spies.</div>
		</div>
	</details>

	<details class="section">
		<summary>Groveling</summary>
		<div class="details-content">
			<div class="faction-desc">Mysterious living entities that do not even know their own destiny on the wheel of fate. Either divine intervention channeled through flora by parasitic symbiosis, a fungal colony combined with tree barks, vines and growths no different from kneestingers on Fog Islands after the fall of magic. Not even elvenkind knows how they came to be, some believe they are revengeful spirits that slipped through Necra's fingers, are Dendor's wraith made form, or an Abyssor's manifestation of symbiosis.</div>
			<div class="faction-desc">Grovelings are parasitic, able to choose a host tree to absorb its nutrients, and anchor itself on the ground to heal and receive less damage from attacks at the cost of not moving at all, and cannot get diseased. However, they are vulnerable to fire and receive semi-permanent damage when their host tree dies. Nonetheless, they have been formed in fungal flesh and fiber with little to no memory of why and how, but they have an unconscious hate for the demonic, including tieflings.</div>
		</div>
	</details>

	<details class="section">
		<summary>Aasimar</summary>
		<div class="details-content">
		<div class="faction-desc">Immortal offspring created through unknown means by command of the goddess Astrata, used as soldiers to fight in the Apotheosis God-War. They quickly earned scorn and fear from the mortal races they fought alongside for their indifference to the suffering of their allies and unquestioning brutality. The aasimar who survived the war have been abandoned by Astrata, left to face an uncertain fate alongside the other races of Grimoria.</div>
		<div class="faction-desc">Apotheosis and memories of their violent legacy have faded, but many still view these aasimar survivors as emotionally dull, stubborn, and simple-minded brutes... though this is not always the case. Aasimar are known for their incredible strength and resilience, and are a prized addition to any shield wall. However, they possess less capacity for independent thought due to their wartime construction.</div>
